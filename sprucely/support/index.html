Help with Sprucely for iPhone

Sprucely can't see my photos

Open iPhone Settings, Apps, Sprucely, Photos, and choose Full Access. With Limited Access, Sprucely only sees the photos you selected.

I deleted something by mistake

Open the Photos app, Collections, Recently Deleted. Deleted items stay there for 30 days and can be recovered.

Why are sizes marked "about"?

Sprucely estimates each photo and video's size from its resolution and length, so totals are close but not exact.

Restore Sprucely Pro

Open Settings in Sprucely and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

The monthly reminder did not arrive

Check iPhone Settings, Notifications, Sprucely, and make sure notifications are on.

Sprucely Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026 · Arman Khan, Melbourne, Australia

Sprucely helps you clear photos and videos you no longer want. There is no account, no sign-up, and we run no servers for it.

Your photos stay on your iPhone

Sprucely reads your photo library on your iPhone only, with the access you grant (all photos or selected photos). Photos and videos are never uploaded, copied off the device, or sent to us or anyone else. Finding similar photos happens on your iPhone.

Deleting

Nothing is deleted until you confirm. iOS asks you to confirm each delete, and deleted items stay in Photos, Recently Deleted, for 30 days.

What the app stores

Sprucely stores your review progress, your bin, the space you have freed and your settings on your iPhone only. Deleting the app deletes them.

Reminders

If you turn on the monthly reminder and allow notifications, the app schedules a local reminder on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to us.

Purchases

Sprucely Pro is a one-off purchase handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Advertising and analytics

Sprucely shows no ads and contains no analytics or tracking tools. We collect no data.

Children

Sprucely is a general-audience app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

We handle information under the Australian Privacy Act 1988. Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing to access or delete on our side.

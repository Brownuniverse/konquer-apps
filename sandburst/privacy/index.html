Sandburst Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026 · Arman Khan, Melbourne, Australia

Sandburst is a puzzle game. We built it so that it works without knowing who you are. There is no account and no sign-up.

What stays on your device

Your coins, levels, scores, streak and settings are saved on your iPhone only. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your name, card or Apple ID. Apple tells the app only that a purchase went through, so we can add your coins.

Game Center (optional)

If you are signed in to Game Center, your Game Center nickname and scores appear on Sandburst leaderboards. This is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Invite codes

If you enter a friend's invite code, the app stores one record in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) database: the code, and a random identifier Apple generates for this app on your device. It is used once, to give you and your friend your coins, and to stop the same device using several codes. It contains no name, email, phone number or contacts.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local reminders on your device (for example "Today's Daily is ready"). Nothing is sent to us.

Advertising

From version 1.0.1, Sandburst shows ads between some games and offers optional videos you can watch for a reward. Ads are served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google receives your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), a device identifier for this app, your approximate location from your IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google acts under its own privacy policy: how Google uses data in ads.

The app asks your permission before any tracking, as Apple requires. If you say no, you still see ads, but they are not based on your activity in other apps. You can change this any time in iPhone Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking. Buying Remove Ads stops the ads between games.

Analytics

The app contains no analytics tools of its own.

Children

Sandburst is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

We handle information under the Australian Privacy Act 1988. Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing to access or delete on our side. To remove an invite-code record, email us and we will delete it.

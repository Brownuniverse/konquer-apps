How do I play?

Drag to move the block, tap to rotate, flick down to drop. Blocks turn into sand. Connect one colour from the left wall to the right wall to clear it. Clears that set off more clears are chains, and chains multiply your score.

I got a new phone. Where are my purchases?

Open Settings in the game and tap "Restore purchases". Coins and level progress are saved on the device, so they don't move between phones.

What is the Daily?

Everyone gets the same blocks each day (UTC). Your first run each day counts for the leaderboard and your streak. Missed a day? A streak shield saves your streak once.

How do invite codes work?

Find your code in Settings. When a friend enters it, you both get 200 coins.

About

Sandburst is a block puzzle where every block you drop dissolves into real flowing sand. Each grain is simulated on its own, so sand piles, slides and settles, and no two clears look the same. Stretch one colour from the left wall to the right wall and the whole band dissolves. The sand that falls can bridge new colours, setting off chain reactions that multiply your score.

Features

  • Per-grain sand physics written in C, deterministic at 60 fps
  • The Daily: identical blocks for every player each day, with streaks and a shareable result grid
  • One-tap 10-second replay video of your best moment, made for TikTok and Reels
  • Original ASMR sound design and three soundtracks: Calm, Pumped and Arcade
  • 100 levels with boss stages, plus an Endless mode that speeds up and adds colours
  • Collectable skins: gemstones, diamond, neon glow, river pebbles and more
  • No account, plays offline, colour-blind friendly palette

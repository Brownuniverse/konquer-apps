Help with Sleeps Left for iPhone

Adding a widget

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Sleeps Left.

Choosing which countdown a widget shows

Touch and hold the widget and tap Edit Widget. If you leave it empty it always shows the next one.

A reminder did not arrive

Check iPhone Settings, Notifications, Sleeps Left, and make sure notifications are on.

Sleeps Left Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026 · Arman Khan, Melbourne, Australia

Sleeps Left counts down to the dates you care about. There is no account, no sign-up, and we run no servers for it.

What stays on your device

Your countdowns and settings are stored on your iPhone only, and shared with the app's own widgets. Deleting the app deletes them. If you back up your iPhone with Apple, they may be included in that backup, under Apple's privacy policy.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local reminders on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to us.

Sharing

A countdown picture leaves your iPhone only when you choose to send it, through the app you pick.

Advertising and analytics

This version shows no ads and contains no analytics or tracking tools. We collect no data.

Children

Sleeps Left is a general-audience app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

We handle information under the Australian Privacy Act 1988. Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing to access or delete on our side.

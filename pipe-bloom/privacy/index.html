Pipe Bloom Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026 · Arman Khan, Melbourne, Australia

Pipe Bloom is a puzzle game where you turn pipes to water a garden. There is no account, no sign-up, and we run no servers for it.

What stays on your device

Your level progress, the board you are playing and your hint count are stored on your iPhone only. Deleting the app deletes them. If you back up your iPhone with Apple, they may be included in that backup, under Apple's privacy policy.

Purchases

Pipe Bloom Pro is bought through Apple. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details. The app only asks Apple whether Pro is owned.

Advertising and analytics

This version shows no ads and contains no analytics or tracking tools. We collect no data.

Children

Pipe Bloom is a general-audience app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

We handle information under the Australian Privacy Act 1988. Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing to access or delete on our side.

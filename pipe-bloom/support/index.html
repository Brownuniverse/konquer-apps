Pipe Bloom Support

Help with Pipe Bloom for iPhone

How to play

Tap a tile to turn it a quarter turn. Water runs from the well through pipes that meet. A level is solved when every flower is watered and no pipe is left open.

I'm stuck

Tap Hint to fix one tile. You get 3 free hints a day; Pro gives unlimited hints. Shuffle starts the same garden again from a new mix.

Restoring Pro

On the home screen, tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID that bought Pro.

Moving to a new iPhone

Progress moves with an iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone. Pro comes back with Restore purchase.

Docket Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026 · Arman Khan, Melbourne, Australia

Docket keeps your receipts and warranty dates. There is no account, no sign-up, and we run no servers for it.

What stays on your device

Your receipts, photos, item details, notes and settings are stored on your iPhone only. Deleting the app deletes them. If you back up your iPhone with Apple, they may be included in that backup, under Apple's privacy policy.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you choose Scan. If you choose a photo, only that photo is given to the app. Reading text from a receipt happens on your iPhone.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local reminders on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to us.

Sharing

A receipt leaves your iPhone only when you choose to send it, through the app you pick.

Advertising and analytics

This version shows no ads and contains no analytics or tracking tools. We collect no data.

Children

Docket is a general-audience app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

We handle information under the Australian Privacy Act 1988. Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing to access or delete on our side.

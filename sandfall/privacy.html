Sandfall Privacy Policy

Effective 24 October 2026 · Konquer Media Pty Ltd, Melbourne, Australia

Sandfall is a puzzle game. We built it so that it works without knowing who you are. There is no account and no sign-up.

What stays on your device

Your coins, levels, scores, streak and settings are saved on your iPhone only. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your name, card or Apple ID. Apple tells the app only that a purchase went through, so we can add your coins.

Game Center (optional)

If you are signed in to Game Center, your Game Center nickname and scores appear on Sandfall leaderboards. This is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Invite codes

If you enter a friend's invite code, the app stores one record in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) database: the code, and a random identifier Apple generates for this app on your device. It is used once, to give you and your friend your coins, and to stop the same device using several codes. It contains no name, email, phone number or contacts.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local reminders on your device (for example "Today's Daily is ready"). Nothing is sent to us.

Advertising and analytics

This version shows no ads and contains no analytics or tracking tools. If a future version adds ads, we will update this policy before that version is released, and the app will ask your permission before any tracking, as Apple requires.

Children

Sandfall is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

We handle information under the Australian Privacy Act 1988. Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing to access or delete on our side. To remove an invite-code record, email us and we will delete it.

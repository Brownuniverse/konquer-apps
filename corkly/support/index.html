Corkly Support

Help with Corkly for iPhone.

Add a wine

Tap + on the Journal tab. Only the name is needed. Add a label photo with Take photo or Choose photo, tap the stars to rate (the left half of a star gives a half star), pick the type, and add as much or as little as you like: tags, body, sweetness, acidity, tannin, grapes, country, region, price, where you had it and notes. Tap Save.

Non-vintage wines

In the Vintage field, type a year like 2019, or tap NV (non-vintage).

Tasted it again

Open the wine and tap Tasted again. Give it a new rating, date and note. The wine shows its latest rating, and every tasting is listed at the bottom of its page. A tasting can be deleted with the bin icon next to it (Corkly asks first).

Buy again

Open a wine and tap Buy again, or turn on Buy again in the form. The Buy again tab lists those wines with the label, producer, vintage, where you got it and the price, so you can show it in the shop.

Find a wine

On the Journal tab, search by name, producer, grape, region or any word in your notes. Tap a type chip to show only that type, and the arrows button to sort by date, rating, price or vintage.

Edit or delete

Open the wine and tap Edit, or use the ... menu for Edit and Delete wine. Corkly asks before deleting. Settings > Erase all data deletes everything (it asks first).

The camera doesn't open

If you turned the camera off for Corkly, open iPhone Settings, Apps, Corkly, and turn on Camera. You can always use Choose photo instead, which needs no permission.

Currency

Prices use your iPhone's region currency. Change it in iPhone Settings, General, Language & Region.

Corkly Pro

Free keeps 20 wines. Corkly Pro is one payment: unlimited wines, full stats (top grapes, top countries and a monthly chart), CSV export of your journal, and your own tasting tags. To restore it, open Settings in Corkly and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

Where is my data?

Only on your iPhone. Corkly has no account and no server. If you back up your iPhone, your journal is part of that backup.

Does Corkly ask for reviews?

Rarely: at most 3 times a year, only right after you save a new wine from your third on, and never in your first session.

Please drink responsibly.

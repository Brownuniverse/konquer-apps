Corkly Privacy Policy

Effective 27 November 2026 · Arman Khan, Melbourne, Australia

Corkly is a private wine tasting journal. There is no account, no sign-up, and we run no servers for it.

What stays on your device

The wines, ratings, tastings, tasting notes, prices, places, custom tags, label photos and settings you enter are stored on your iPhone only. Deleting the app, or Erase all data in Settings, deletes them. If you back up your iPhone with Apple, they may be included in that backup, under Apple's privacy policy.

Camera and photos

Corkly uses the camera only when you tap Take photo, to photograph a wine label for your journal. Choose photo uses Apple's photo picker, which gives Corkly only the photo you pick; Corkly cannot see the rest of your library. Label photos are made smaller and kept with your journal on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded.

Exporting

When you export your journal as a CSV file (Corkly Pro), the file goes only where you send it, using Apple's share sheet.

Purchases

Corkly Pro is a one-off purchase handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Advertising and analytics

Corkly shows no ads and contains no analytics or tracking tools. We collect no data.

Age

Corkly is a journal for adults of legal drinking age. It does not sell alcohol. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

We handle information under the Australian Privacy Act 1988. Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing to access or delete on our side.

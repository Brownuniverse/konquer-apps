Kitbag Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026 · Arman Khan, Melbourne, Australia

Kitbag builds packing lists for your trips. There is no account, no sign-up, and we run no servers for it.

What stays on your device

Your trips, destinations, dates, packing lists, essentials, templates and settings are stored on your iPhone only. Deleting the app deletes them. If you back up your iPhone with Apple, they may be included in that backup, under Apple's privacy policy.

Reminders

If you turn on the "Pack tonight" reminder and allow notifications, the app schedules local reminders on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to us.

Widget

The Home Screen widget reads your next trip from your iPhone.

Sharing and printing

When you choose to share or print a list, it goes only where you send it, using Apple's share sheet or printing.

Purchases

Kitbag Pro is a one-off purchase handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Advertising and analytics

Kitbag shows no ads and contains no analytics or tracking tools. We collect no data.

Children

Kitbag is a general-audience app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

We handle information under the Australian Privacy Act 1988. Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing to access or delete on our side.

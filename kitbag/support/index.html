Help with Kitbag for iPhone

How is my list made?

Kitbag picks items from its built-in list of about 200 travel items, based on the kinds of trip you choose (beach, city, business, hiking and camping, snow, with kids, with a baby, flying, road trip, gym and sport) and the number of nights. Clothes are scaled to your stay, and about halved on longer trips when laundry is available. Everything can be edited.

Add, edit or move an item

Type in the "Add an item" box at the bottom of a trip. Kitbag guesses the category; tap the icon next to the box to choose one. Swipe an item left to edit or delete it, or touch and hold it to move it to another category.

My essentials

Open Settings, then My essentials, to list things you always bring. They are added to every new trip.

Packing for the way home

In a trip, tap "Repack for home". Every tick is cleared so the same list works on the way back.

Pack the same trip again

Touch and hold a past trip on the Trips screen and choose "Duplicate as a new trip", or use the ... menu inside a trip.

Reminders don't arrive

In Kitbag, open Settings and turn on "Pack tonight reminder". If iPhone notifications are off for Kitbag, open iPhone Settings, Apps, Kitbag, Notifications, and turn on Allow Notifications. The reminder only comes the evening before a trip that still has things to pack.

Add the widget

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Kitbag. It shows your next trip, the days to go and what's left to pack.

Kitbag Pro

Free holds 2 upcoming trips at a time. Kitbag Pro is one payment: unlimited trips, your own templates, and share or print. To restore it, open Settings in Kitbag and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

Moving to a new iPhone

Your trips move with an iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone.

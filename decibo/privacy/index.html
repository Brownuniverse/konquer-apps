Decibo Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026 · Arman Khan, Melbourne, Australia

Decibo is a sound level meter for iPhone. There is no account, no sign-up, and we run no servers for it.

Microphone

Decibo uses the microphone only to measure how loud it is. The sound is turned into level numbers (decibels) on your iPhone as it comes in, then discarded. Audio is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. You can turn microphone access off at any time in iPhone Settings, Apps, Decibo.

What stays on your device

Saved sessions (their names, dates and level readings) and your settings are stored on your iPhone only. Deleting the app deletes them. If you back up your iPhone with Apple, they may be included in that backup, under Apple's privacy policy.

Widget

The Home Screen widget shows your last saved session, read from your iPhone.

Purchases

Decibo Pro is a one-off purchase handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Advertising and analytics

Decibo shows no ads and contains no analytics or tracking tools. We collect no data.

Children

Decibo is a general-audience app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

We handle information under the Australian Privacy Act 1988. Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing to access or delete on our side.

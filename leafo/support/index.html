Help with Leafo for iPhone

How often should I water?

When you pick a plant type, Leafo suggests a schedule for a typical home. It is a starting point: light, pot size and season change how fast soil dries. Check the soil with a finger, and change the schedule on the plant's Edit screen.

The soil is still damp

On a thirsty plant, tap "Soil's still damp: remind me tomorrow", or touch and hold it on the Today screen.

I watered on another day

Open the plant, tap the ... menu, then "Watered on another day". To remove a wrong entry, touch and hold it in the plant's history.

Reminders don't arrive

In Leafo, open Settings and turn on Watering reminders. If iPhone notifications are off for Leafo, open iPhone Settings, Apps, Leafo, Notifications, and turn on Allow Notifications. Leafo only sends a reminder on days a plant needs care.

Add the widget

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Leafo. Tap the drop on the widget to mark a plant as watered.

Camera access

Open iPhone Settings, Apps, Leafo, and turn on Camera. You can also choose a photo from your library.

Restore Leafo Pro

Open Settings in Leafo and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

Moving to a new iPhone

Your plants move with an iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone.

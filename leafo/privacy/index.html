Leafo Privacy Policy

Effective 20 November 2026 · Arman Khan, Melbourne, Australia

Leafo reminds you when to water your plants. There is no account, no sign-up, and we run no servers for it.

What stays on your device

Your plants, their names, rooms, schedules, notes, photos, care history and settings are stored on your iPhone only. Deleting the app deletes them. If you back up your iPhone with Apple, they may be included in that backup, under Apple's privacy policy.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you choose to take a photo of a plant. If you choose a photo from your library, only that photo is given to the app. Photos stay on your iPhone.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders and allow notifications, the app schedules local reminders on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to us.

Widget

The Home Screen widget reads your plant list from your iPhone. Watering from the widget is saved on your iPhone.

Purchases

Leafo Pro is a one-off purchase handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Advertising and analytics

Leafo shows no ads and contains no analytics or tracking tools. We collect no data.

Children

Leafo is a general-audience app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

We handle information under the Australian Privacy Act 1988. Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing to access or delete on our side.

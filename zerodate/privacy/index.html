Zerodate Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026 · Arman Khan, Melbourne, Australia

Zerodate plans how to pay off your debts. There is no account, no sign-up, no bank linking, and we run no servers for it.

What stays on your device

The debts, balances, interest rates, payments, notes and settings you enter are stored on your iPhone only. Deleting the app, or Erase all data in Settings, deletes them. If you back up your iPhone with Apple, they may be included in that backup, under Apple's privacy policy.

Reminders

If you turn on payment-due reminders and allow notifications, the app schedules local reminders on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to us.

Widgets

The Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets read your plan from your iPhone.

Exporting

When you export a schedule, the file goes only where you send it, using Apple's share sheet.

Purchases

Zerodate Pro is a one-off purchase handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Advertising and analytics

Zerodate shows no ads and contains no analytics or tracking tools. We collect no data.

Children

Zerodate is a general-audience app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

We handle information under the Australian Privacy Act 1988. Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing to access or delete on our side.

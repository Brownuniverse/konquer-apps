Convly Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026 · Arman Khan, Melbourne, Australia

Convly converts units of measurement. There is no account, no sign-up, and we run no servers for it.

What stays on your device

Your pinned conversions, history, custom units and settings are stored on your iPhone only. Deleting the app deletes them. If you back up your iPhone with Apple, they may be included in that backup, under Apple's privacy policy.

Widget

The Home Screen widget reads your pinned conversions from your iPhone.

Copying

When you tap a result, Convly puts it on your iPhone's clipboard so you can paste it. Convly does not read your clipboard.

Purchases

Convly Pro is a one-off purchase handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Advertising and analytics

Convly shows no ads and contains no analytics or tracking tools. It makes no network requests apart from Apple's in-app purchase system. We collect no data.

Children

Convly is a general-audience app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

We handle information under the Australian Privacy Act 1988. Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing to access or delete on our side.

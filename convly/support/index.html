Help with Convly for iPhone

Convert a value

Pick a category from the row of chips (or the grid button for all of them), tap the number and type. Every unit in the category updates as you type. Tap the unit under the number to change the unit you type in. "A to B" shows one big result, with a button to swap the units.

Copy a result

Tap any result to copy it. Touch and hold a result for more options, such as converting from that unit. In Settings you can choose whether the unit is copied with the number.

Pins

Tap the pin icon on a result to keep that conversion at the top of the app and on the widget. Tap Edit next to Pinned to reorder, change or remove pins.

Cups to grams

In Cooking, choose the ingredient. Cups and spoons turn into grams using a typical weight for that ingredient, spooned and levelled. For exact baking, a kitchen scale is best.

Fuel economy and number bases

Fuel converts L/100 km, km/L and miles per gallon (US and UK) both ways. Bases converts between decimal, hexadecimal, binary and octal.

Too many or too few decimals

Open Settings and change Significant digits. Very large and very small numbers switch to powers of ten.

Add the widget

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Convly. It shows your first pins.

Currency

Convly works fully offline, so it does not convert currencies, whose rates change every day.

Convly Pro

Every conversion is free with no limit. Convly Pro is one payment for extras: unlimited pins (free keeps 4), your own units, full history (free shows the last 20), 6 pins on the large widget and widget themes. To restore it, open Settings in Convly and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

Leaky Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026 · Arman Khan, Melbourne, Australia

Leaky keeps track of your subscriptions and reminds you before they renew. There is no account, no sign-up, no bank login, and we run no servers for it.

What stays on your device

The subscriptions you add, their prices, dates, notes and settings are stored on your iPhone only. The Home Screen widget reads them from the same device. Leaky never connects to your bank. Deleting the app deletes your data. If you back up your iPhone with Apple, it may be included in that backup, under Apple's privacy policy.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local reminders on your iPhone before renewals and before free trials end. Nothing is sent to us.

Export

A CSV file leaves your iPhone only when you choose to share it, through the app you pick.

Purchases

Leaky Pro is a one-off purchase handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Advertising and analytics

Leaky shows no ads and contains no analytics or tracking tools. We collect no data.

Children

Leaky is a general-audience app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

We handle information under the Australian Privacy Act 1988. Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing to access or delete on our side.

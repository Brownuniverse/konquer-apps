Help with Leaky for iPhone

Are the suggested prices right?

Quick-pick prices are typical figures to get you started. Tap a subscription to change the price to what you actually pay.

A renewal reminder did not arrive

Check iPhone Settings, Notifications, Leaky, and make sure notifications are on. Check the next billing date on the subscription too.

Adding the widget

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Leaky.

Restore Leaky Pro

Open Settings in Leaky and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

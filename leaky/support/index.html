Help with Leaky for iPhone

How does Leaky find my subscriptions?

You add them yourself, from the list of common services or your own. Leaky never asks for a bank login, so nothing is read from your accounts.

How is the monthly total worked out?

Weekly, yearly and other billing cycles are converted to a monthly and a yearly amount and added up. Paused and cancelled subscriptions are left out of the total.

Cancelling a subscription

Leaky can't cancel for you. Subscriptions billed by Apple are in iPhone Settings, your name, Subscriptions. For others, open the service's website or app. Then mark it cancelled in Leaky to see what you save.

Notifications

Renewal and free-trial reminders need notifications. Open iPhone Settings, Apps, Leaky, Notifications, and turn on Allow Notifications.

Restore Leaky Pro

Open Settings in Leaky and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

Moving to a new iPhone

Your list moves with an iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone. You can also export it as a CSV file.

Clockpay Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026 · Arman Khan, Melbourne, Australia

Clockpay tracks the hours you work and what you earn. There is no account, no sign-up, and we run no servers for it.

What stays on your device

The jobs, hourly rates, shifts, breaks, notes, public holiday dates and settings you enter are stored on your iPhone only. Deleting the app, or Erase all data in Settings, deletes them. If you back up your iPhone with Apple, they may be included in that backup, under Apple's privacy policy.

Reminder

If you turn on the "Still clocked in?" reminder and allow notifications, the app schedules one local notification on your iPhone while a shift is running. Nothing is sent to us.

Widgets

The Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets read your shifts from your iPhone, and their Clock in / Clock out button changes them on your iPhone.

Exporting

When you export a timesheet as a PDF or CSV, the file goes only where you send it, using Apple's share sheet.

Purchases

Clockpay Pro is a one-off purchase handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Advertising and analytics

Clockpay shows no ads and contains no analytics or tracking tools. We collect no data.

Children

Clockpay is a general-audience app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

We handle information under the Australian Privacy Act 1988. Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing to access or delete on our side.

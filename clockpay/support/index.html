Help with Clockpay for iPhone

How is my pay worked out?

Clockpay takes the time from clock in to clock out, takes off unpaid breaks, applies any rounding you chose for the job, and multiplies the paid time by the job's hourly rate. Shifts across midnight and daylight saving changes count the real hours worked. With Clockpay Pro, overtime and penalty rates are added: each minute is paid at the higher of its overtime or weekend and public holiday rate; they don't stack. Check your award or contract for your exact rates.

I forgot to clock in

While clocked in, tap Edit next to "Started" on the Clock tab and move the start back, with the quick buttons or by picking a time. If the shift is already over, use "Add a past shift" instead.

Overnight shifts

When adding a past shift, set an end time earlier than the start time (for example 10:00 pm to 6:00 am). Clockpay knows it ends the next day and says so in the form.

Breaks

Tap Break while clocked in, and End break when you're back. Breaks are unpaid by default; turn on "Paid breaks" in the job if yours are paid. For past shifts, choose the break minutes in the form.

Weeks and pay periods

The Clock tab and the all-jobs timesheet use weeks starting on the day set in Settings. Each job can also have its own pay period, weekly, fortnightly or monthly; tap the job's name on the Timesheet tab to see its periods and totals.

Edit or delete a shift

Tap any shift to change its date, times, break or note, or to delete it. On the Timesheet tab you can also swipe left on a shift. Clockpay asks before deleting, and offers Undo straight after.

The "Still clocked in?" reminder doesn't arrive

In Clockpay, open Settings and turn on the reminder. If iPhone notifications are off for Clockpay, open iPhone Settings, Apps, Clockpay, Notifications, and turn on Allow Notifications.

Add the widget

Touch and hold your Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit (or Customise), then Add Widget, and choose Clockpay. Its button clocks you in or out without opening the app, and it shows the running shift and this week's hours and pay.

Clockpay Pro

Free covers 2 jobs. Clockpay Pro is one payment: unlimited jobs, overtime rules, Saturday, Sunday and public holiday rates, and PDF or CSV timesheet export. To restore it, open Settings in Clockpay and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

Does Clockpay ask for reviews?

Almost never: at most 3 times a year, only right after you finish a shift, and never in your first session.

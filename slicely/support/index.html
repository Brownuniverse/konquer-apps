Help with Slicely for iPhone

The scan read an item wrong

Reading a receipt is a best guess. Tap any item to fix its name or price, or add and delete items. If the items don't add up to the receipt, Slicely shows the difference.

The totals are a cent off

Slicely shares out leftover cents so everyone's totals add up exactly to the bill.

Camera access

Open iPhone Settings, Apps, Slicely, and turn on Camera. You can also pick a photo or type items instead.

Restore Slicely Pro

Open Settings in Slicely and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

Moving to a new iPhone

Your bills move with an iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone.

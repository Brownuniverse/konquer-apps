Slicely Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026 · Arman Khan, Melbourne, Australia

Slicely splits a bill between friends. There is no account, no sign-up, and we run no servers for it.

What stays on your device

Your bills, items, the names you add, payments marked as paid, and settings are stored on your iPhone only. Deleting the app deletes them. If you back up your iPhone with Apple, they may be included in that backup, under Apple's privacy policy.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you choose to scan a receipt. If you pick a photo, only that photo is given to the app. Reading the receipt happens on your iPhone.

Names

Slicely does not read your contacts. The names you type are kept on your iPhone to suggest them next time.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders and allow notifications, the app schedules local reminders on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to us.

Sharing

A summary leaves your iPhone only when you choose to share it, through the app you pick.

Purchases

Slicely Pro is a one-off purchase handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Advertising and analytics

Slicely shows no ads and contains no analytics or tracking tools. We collect no data.

Children

Slicely is a general-audience app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

We handle information under the Australian Privacy Act 1988. Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing to access or delete on our side.

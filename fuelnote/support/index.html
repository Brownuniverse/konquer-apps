Help with Fuelnote for iPhone

Why is there no economy figure yet?

Fuel economy is worked out between two full tanks. Log your next full fill-up and the figure appears.

I missed logging a fill-up

Mark the next fill-up as "missed one before" so Fuelnote does not count the gap as one tank.

Changing units

Open Settings in Fuelnote to switch between km and litres, miles and US gallons, or miles and litres.

Restore Fuelnote Pro

Open Settings in Fuelnote and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

A reminder did not arrive

Check iPhone Settings, Notifications, Fuelnote, and make sure notifications are on.

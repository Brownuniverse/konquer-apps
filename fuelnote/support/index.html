Help with Fuelnote for iPhone

Why is there no economy figure yet?

Fuel economy needs two fill-ups: Fuelnote measures the distance you drove since the last full tank and the fuel it took to fill up again. Log your next fill-up and the figure appears.

I didn't fill the tank to the top

Turn off "Full tank" for that fill-up. Fuelnote adds the fuel to the next full tank, so the figure stays right.

I forgot to log a fill-up

Turn on "Missed a fill-up" for the next one. Fuelnote then starts a new measurement instead of showing a wrong figure.

Changing units

Open Settings in Fuelnote to switch between litres and gallons, kilometres and miles, and the economy format you prefer.

Notifications

Service reminders need notifications. Open iPhone Settings, Apps, Fuelnote, Notifications, and turn on Allow Notifications.

Restore Fuelnote Pro

Open Settings in Fuelnote and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

Moving to a new iPhone

Your log moves with an iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone. You can also export it as a CSV file.

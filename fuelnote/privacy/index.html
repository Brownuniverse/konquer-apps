Fuelnote Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026 · Arman Khan, Melbourne, Australia

Fuelnote is a fuel log for your car. There is no account, no sign-up, and we run no servers for it.

What stays on your device

Your vehicles, fill-ups, odometer readings, costs, service reminders and settings are stored on your iPhone only. Deleting the app deletes them. If you back up your iPhone with Apple, they may be included in that backup, under Apple's privacy policy.

Location

Fuelnote does not use your location.

Reminders

If you turn on a service reminder and allow notifications, the app schedules local reminders on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to us.

Export

Your log leaves your iPhone only when you choose to export it, through the app you pick.

Purchases

Fuelnote Pro is a one-off purchase handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Advertising and analytics

Fuelnote shows no ads and contains no analytics or tracking tools. We collect no data.

Children

Fuelnote is a general-audience app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

We handle information under the Australian Privacy Act 1988. Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing to access or delete on our side.
